website. Change ONLY the mouth: replace the neutral closed mouth with a small,
warm, slightly open smile, a very narrow glimpse of upper teeth. Restrained
friendly smile, not laughing or a broad grin. Keep the eyes, glasses, face outline,
head position, nose, hair, purple hood, hoodie logo, texture, colors, white
background, scale and framing exactly as in the source. Preserve the full original
nearly square composition and every feature outside the mouth so switching images
does not make the avatar jump. No added details or text. Output one edited image.
